## 💡 About The Project

Over 80% of neurodivergent adults face unemployment or underemployment, despite possessing exceptional pattern recognition, high attention to detail, and systematic thinking suited for high-tech industries. Meanwhile, the **Polymer & Plastics Recycling (RCER - Recycling & Circular Economy Resources)** sector is rapidly expanding but lacks accessible entry pathways.

**Neurodivergent Space** provides a sensory-friendly, accessible web environment for neurodivergent individuals, parents, caregivers, and vocational advocates to evaluate workplace readiness, explore sustainable manufacturing careers, locate accredited college programs, and secure financial aid.

---

## ⚙️ Core Features

1. **0–10 Functional Assessment Scale**: Evaluates Independence, Sensory Tolerance, Instructional Support, Safety Needs, and Social Preferences with explicit 0 vs 10 endpoint clarity anchors.
2. **Real-Time Radar Chart & PDF Exporter**: Generates structured IEP & transition support summaries.
3. **Annual Student Support Booklet (RCER)**: Direct links to official state health portals (IDPH) for required annual health forms.
4. **Sensory Overload & De-Escalation Kit**: Non-verbal Advocacy Card, interactive 4-7-8 Box Breathing guide, and soundscape masking tools.
5. **Spoon Theory Energy Budget Planner**: Allows users to budget daily sensory energy and avoid burnout.
6. **Skills & Supported Activities Tracker**: Categorizes tasks into Independent, Prompted, Supervised, or Direct Assistance levels.
